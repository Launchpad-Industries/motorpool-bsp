About Motor Pool

Motor Pool is an unofficial fan-made force builder for BattleTech Battlefield Support assets. Search for units, build a force, and print its cards.

Last updated 10 November 2026. The calculations are still being tuned, so this page describes how they work today.

Where each card comes from

OfficialStats as printed by Catalyst Game Labs. Used whenever a card exists, unless you switch a force entry to Calc.
ApprovedCalculated and possibly tweaked, then approved by someone whose signature this app trusts.
UnverifiedApproved under someone's name, but without a signature this app trusts, so treat it as that person's opinion.
CalculatedWorked out by the formulas below and not reviewed by anyone.

What's listed

  • Vehicles: every combat vehicle and VTOL in MegaMek's data, plus support vehicles that have an official card.
  • Battle armor: squads MegaMek's force generator gives to at least one faction, at standard sizes: 5 suits for Clan, 4 for Inner Sphere, and 6 for the versions it lists (nearly all ComStar and Word of Blake). A design available only at another size keeps that size.
  • Infantry and emplacements: the generic official cards only, for now.
  • Strikes: air, artillery and minefield options from the Core Rulebook, in our own words.

How a vehicle becomes a card

MPThe unit's cruise speed.
TMMThe standard Total Warfare movement table, +1 for VTOL, WiGE or stealth.
Skill6, or 5 for Clan front-line designs. One step better with a targeting computer. 8 for artillery.
RangeThe weapon type worth the most Battle Value (BV) sets the range, using the largest standard bracket whose medium range it reaches. If that weapon fires indirectly, a strong direct-fire weapon can set it instead.
DamageThe average damage of the main weapons. Cluster weapons count 0.6 of their missiles or pellets (×1.2 with Artemis IV), and Streaks count in full. Weapons that fall well short on range or value are left out, one reason assets hit less hard than their full units. The total is split into groups, usually of 5, or larger when big guns carry enough of it.
CheckAn armor score, 2.5 × armor + 1.5 × structure, adjusted for armor type and movement (hover and WiGE lowest, tracked highest), then matched to a Check level.
Threshold8 at 75 tons or more, otherwise 5.
SpecialsAdded from the unit's equipment, such as ECM, TAG, IF, APC, Commander, AMS, Crit-Seeker and No Turret.

What's different for battle armor

  • Movement: jump MP if the suit has jump jets, otherwise walking MP. TMM gets +1 for jumping and +1 for stealth or mimetic armor.
  • Weapons: weapons with 2 shots or fewer don't count, unless the suit would otherwise have no attack.
  • Damage: one suit's damage × the squad's average cluster hits (2.64 for 4 suits, 3.17 for 5, 4.00 for 6), counted twice, as on the official cards. A squad lands many small hits, so the total is printed in groups of no more than 6 (or the size of one suit's biggest gun, up to 10).
  • Check and Threshold: the same armor score, × 2.8 instead of a movement adjustment. Threshold is always 5.

How cost works

Cost is worked out from the card's own stats, not from the unit's Total Warfare BV. The numbers were fitted to the official vehicle cards.

Regular = (7.5 + (offense + defense) ÷ 20 + flat specials)
          × 1.15 for each point of Skill better than 6
Veteran = the unrounded Regular cost × 1.15

Offense = 9.7 × speed × damage × (long range ÷ 9)^0.6 × specials
Defense = 0.6 × Check value × TMM factor × special armor
  • Skill: a better Skill is priced exactly like Veteran, since both are one point better in play: a Skill 5 Regular costs what a Skill 6 Veteran with the same stats costs. Artillery, at Skill 8, instead has its offense divided by 1.1 per point.
  • Speed: grows with MP, for example 0.77 at MP 3 and 1.0 at MP 5.
  • Check value: 75 at Check 7, +75 per step above, × 2/3 per step below.
  • TMM factor: how many more attacks it takes to land a hit. TMM 1 to 5 give × 1.10, 1.27, 1.57, 2.2 and 3.3.
  • Offense specials: Crit-Seeker × 1.1, AI × 1.05, No Turret × 0.9.
  • Defense specials: reactive, reflective or ballistic-reinforced armor × 1.5, AMS × 1.15.
  • Flat points: Commander 15, ECM6 7, ECM3 3.5, TAG 2, probe 1, each Arrow IV 38, and each artillery piece 29 to 57. APC costs about 1.1 per level, more on tougher carriers.

BV equivalent in the force totals is simply BSP cost × 20.

Worked example: Elemental [MG], 5 suits

Its calculated card has MP 3, TMM 2, range 1/2/3, damage 6×2, Skill 5 and Check 9.

Offense9.7 × 0.77 × 12 × (3 ÷ 9)^0.6 = 46.4
Defense0.6 × 225 × 1.27 = 171.3
Regular(7.5 + (46.4 + 171.3) ÷ 20) × 1.15 (Skill 5) = 21.1, which rounds to 21
Veteran21.1 × 1.15 = 24.3, which rounds to 24

The official Elemental card has the same stats but costs 17 (20). Catalyst prices its Skill 5 cards a little below its own Veteran rule; Motor Pool prices them the same as a Veteran, so Clan cards come out a few points higher.

Known limits

  • Calculated cards are estimates. Priced from their printed stats, 119 official vehicle cards come out 2.5 points off on average, and 79 are within 2 points.
  • Calculated stats can differ from a printed card too. The Differences view shows where.
  • Battle armor costs are a first pass: only 4 official cards exist to check them against.
  • Some suits have no attack on their card, because they carry only anti-personnel weapons, TAG or equipment the model doesn't cover yet, such as mortars and micro bombs.
  • Special battle armor types, such as reactive and reflective, get no Check bonus yet.
