MOTOR POOL - beta test build
============================

Motor Pool builds BattleTech Battlefield Support (BSP) asset cards from
MegaMek's vehicle and battle armor data, plus the official infantry and
emplacement cards and the air, artillery and minefield strike cards: search
units, build a force, preview the cards and print them (8 per US Letter
page). This is unofficial fan content.

This is a beta: expect rough edges, and please send feedback.


STARTING IT
-----------
1. Unzip the whole folder somewhere, for example your Documents folder.
   (Running it from inside the zip without unzipping won't work.)
2. Double-click MotorPool.exe.
3. The first time, Windows may show a blue box: "Windows protected your PC".
   That's because this small hobby app isn't signed with a paid
   certificate. Click "More info", then "Run anyway".
4. A small black window opens and, a few seconds later, Motor Pool opens in
   your web browser. Everything runs on your own computer; it doesn't need
   the internet.

If the browser doesn't open, copy the address shown in the black window
(it looks like http://127.0.0.1:52345/) into your browser.


QUITTING
--------
Close the black window. (Closing the browser tab alone leaves it running.)


USING IT
--------
- Search in the middle, click a result to see its card on the right, and
  add it to your force on the left.
- Make PDF saves a printable sheet (it uses Microsoft Edge, which comes with
  Windows). Print view opens the same sheet in your browser if you'd rather
  print from there.
- Your version and the data date are shown under the MOTOR POOL title.


SENDING FEEDBACK
----------------
- Card changes: open a card, use Tweak, then "Add to tweaks to send" and
  "Send tweaks for approval". It opens an email to Will with your changes
  already written in; just press Send. (If no email program opens, use
  "Copy text" and paste it into an email.)
- Anything else (bugs, ideas): email Will, or message Launchpad on Discord.
  Please mention the version shown under the title. If something went
  wrong, the black window may show an error message: a copy of that helps.


YOUR DATA
---------
Your settings and any approvals you make are saved in a folder called
.motorpool in your user folder (C:\Users\<you>\.motorpool). Delete the
Motor Pool folder and that one to remove everything.
